Every lap shows up on the laptop, the phones in the pits and the TV on the wall, with personal bests and track records as they happen.

On a track at home, or as your club's timing system.

Set up Timing

Build it, or bring yours

Timing reads a transponder in each car as it passes a decoder at the finish line. Build both yourself, or bring the decoder you already have: an EasyLap, or any decoder that speaks ZRound.

Build your own

A decoder at the finish line and a transponder in each car, running our firmware.

How it goes
  1. 1

    Build the decoder. An ESP32 board with four infrared receivers.

  2. 2

    Mount the receivers on a bridge over the finish line, about 30 cm up.

  3. 3

    Install a transponder in each car.

  4. 4

    Flash our firmware onto the decoder and the transponders. The decoder then sends laps over Wi-Fi.
